Spent years facilitating the relationships and vision that brought Kansas its first Internet Exchange Point — now becoming a national blueprint for AI-ready interconnection. The State of Kansas awarded a $5M matching grant to IXP.US to build and operate the IXP as a WSU Innovation Campus Partner. Read the announcement →

Continuing work with IXP.US to build out the Wichita IXP and assist with expansion across 125 regional hub cities in the United States.

Tonya's role: Convener, partnership architect — connected Connected Nation, DE-CIX, the Kansas Governor's office, and WSU into a working coalition.

Building at Scale · 2018–2025

Wichita State Innovation Campus

As AVP for Industry Engagement & Applied Learning, helped facilitate one of the most successful university-industry workforce models in the country — 700+ employer partnerships, $33M+ in student wages across 9,800+ positions in 2023–24, and 50+ industry partners physically located on the Innovation Campus. Contributed to WSU's #1 U.S. ranking for aerospace engineering R&D ($327M, surpassing Georgia Tech, MIT, and Stanford). UEDA Award of Excellence finalist. AURP Emerging Research Park Award winner. wichita.edu/innovationcampus →

Tonya's role: Industry engagement strategy, employer relationship building, and applied learning architecture — including the launch of the Shocker Career Accelerator, Ennovar, ShockStarter, and the National Institute for Digital Transformation. Led the two-year self-study and application for WSU's designation as an APLU Innovation & Economic Prosperity (IEP) University.

TEAM · Dr. John Bardo · WSU Executive Team · WSU & WSU Tech Faculty and Students · 700+ Industry Employers
Crisis Response · 2020

WSU Molecular Diagnostics Lab

Co-founded and led the build of a CLIA-certified molecular diagnostics laboratory from the ground up in 90 days — delivering 350,000 COVID-19 test results at up to 4,500 tests per day. Kept 600+ businesses and 400+ schools across Kansas operational through the pandemic. Winner of the Wichita Business Journal Innovation Award (2021). GWP feature → · WSU opening release →

Tonya's role: Co-Founder & Executive Director — created the operational plan to distribute specimen collection across the community and deliver 24-hour PCR COVID test results, coordinating partnerships across university, county, state, and private sector.

TEAM · Sarah Nickel · Debbie Franklin · Rob Gerlach · Brian Brown · Dr. Joel Alderson · NIAR · Sedgwick County · State of Kansas · KDHE

As Director, led the digital transformation of legacy university media — moved telecourses from VHS and DVD to fully streaming online, brought all of WSU TV (cable channel 13) programming online with the creation of WSU TV Streaming, and grew KMUW (NPR) audiences by launching podcasts and RSS streaming radio. Foresight in 2012 of what every institution would do a decade later.

Tonya's role: Director — vision, technology strategy, team leadership across WSU TV, KMUW, and Instructional Systems.
