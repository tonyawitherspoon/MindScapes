02 · The Thesis

Connection compounds.

MindScapes connects the layers most organizations build in silos — infrastructure, workforce, and AI — into one architecture for digital transformation. The whole emerges as more than the sum of the parts.

The math is intentional: 2+2=9. Not 2+2=5, the usual shorthand for synergy. Even, even, odd. It's addition plus multiplication, plus one more for the emergence no one saw coming. Connection isn't additive. It's compounding.

The Operating Principle
2 + 2 = 9

The brand is built on one belief: that the connector is the highest-leverage role in any system. Not the inventor. Not the operator. The person who sees across layers and brings the right people to each one. That person creates the conditions for emergence.

05 · What We've Tried

Five rounds in. Honest notes.

Claude and I did five rounds of logo explorations before deciding to focus on language first and bring you in for the visual work. Here's what we tried and where each one landed for me, so you don't repeat the misses.

✕ Didn't land

Brain marks

The literal brain felt clinical. The brain-as-topography felt closer but too biological. I'm not selling neuroscience.

✕ Didn't land

The M sigil

Geometric M with a glowing core node. Steve and I both saw a heart instead of an M. Hard pass.

○ Closest direction

Topographic elevation arcs

Layered curves rising to a convergence point with the emerald-to-cyan gradient. Strategically right. Execution still felt thin — the curves were barely curved, the node looked like a period, the whole thing was too quiet.

○ Interesting concept

The compounding sigil

Three arcs rising and tightening into an apex — visualizing the compounding curve itself. Concept-rich. Execution went a little too "tech diagram."

✓ Probably the right path

Wordmark-led

"Mind" in plain weight + italic gradient "Scapes." No symbol at all, or a very quiet contour beneath. I keep coming back to this — the name is already doing the work.

✓ Need this too

A 25×25 icon

Whatever the main lockup, I need a tiny version that works as a social avatar and favicon — Stripe-, Anthropic-, Linear-style.

06 · The Brief

What I'm hoping you can do.

The Ask

Design a wordmark + small mark for MindScapes.

  • The wordmark is the primary logo. "Mind" + "Scapes" — both capitalized, two words pressed together, the second one likely italic. The brand will live or die by this.
  • A small mark — somewhere between a monogram, a contour, and a node — that works as a 25×25 favicon and social avatar.
  • The lockup — how the wordmark and small mark relate (horizontal, stacked, used independently).
  • The typeface decision. Steve doesn't love Fraunces + Outfit. I trust your eye more than mine on this. Recommend a pairing.
  • Variations — full color on dark, full color on light, single-color, monochrome.

How I'll know it's right

"Everything is connected. It makes sense. I can understand the essence by just looking at the logo." — That's the brief in my own words.

Beyond that: I want a logo that has settings and occasions — that can dress up for a Rockefeller event and dress down for a coffee mug. Like a good piece of jewelry, or a table set with the good china. Layered, not minimal.

What you should ignore

If something Claude and I tried doesn't work for you, ignore it. The language is locked. The colors are locked. The visual execution is wide open — you both have better eyes than the AI, and I trust your judgment more than mine on this part.
