For Andrew, Cait, & Steve · A Typography Review

Five directions for the
MindScapes type system.

The current Fraunces + Outfit pairing has gotten honest pushback from both Steve and Cait. Here are four alternative directions plus the current baseline — same content, same colors, different type. The most important moment in each direction is the wordmark itself — since "MindScapes" may end up being the logo. Scroll, compare, react.

HOW TO USE THIS PAGE: Each direction below shows the same brand content rendered in a different type pairing. Start with the wordmark at the top of each direction — that's the potential logo. Then read down through the supporting moments: hero, brand word "Layers.", section heading, body paragraph, proof card, and italic emphasis. The point is to see them in actual MindScapes content, not lorem ipsum. Reply by email with which direction (or combination) feels right — or what's still off.

Baseline · Current Pairing

Fraunces & Outfit

Display: Fraunces (humanist serif, optical sizing) · Body: Outfit (geometric sans, Google Fonts only)

"Warm artisan." Steve and Cait both flagged it as too soft — Cait specifically said it reminded her of a landscaping brand. Included here as the reference point against which the four alternatives can be judged.

Direction 01 · Editorial Authority

Crimson Pro & Inter

Display: Crimson Pro (contemporary classical serif) · Body: Inter (gold standard for modern UI typography)

"The Atlantic. Stripe. The New York Times' redesign." Editorial gravity + clean precision. Serious without being stuffy. The pair signals — we publish thinking, we deliver execution. Both fonts free on Google Fonts. Claude's recommended pick.

Direction 02 · Modern Operator

Manrope & Inter

Display: Manrope (geometric sans, tightened) · Body: Inter (modern UI standard)

"Anthropic. Linear. Vercel. Arc." Pure geometric sans for both display and body, with contrast through weight rather than typeface. Maximum contemporary confidence. Less editorial warmth — more direct.

Direction 03 · Heritage Architect

Spectral & Manrope

Display: Spectral (contemporary serif designed for screens) · Body: Manrope (geometric sans, warm)

"Cambr. Pentagram. A high-end architecture firm." Sharper than Fraunces, more architectural, less floral. Quietly distinctive. Says "we know what we're doing" without showing off.

Direction 04 · Heartland Modern

Inter (display & body)

Display: Inter Black/ExtraBold (weight contrast) · Body: Inter Regular/Light

"Patagonia's annual report. Bloomberg Terminal. Bridgewater. Field Notes." All-sans, weight does the work. The most distinctive direction — drops the serif moment entirely. Says: confident, American, unfussy. Pairs cleanly with the heartland positioning.
