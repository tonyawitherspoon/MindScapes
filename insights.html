Designations & Recognition

The work beneath the institutional credential.

APLU IEP University Designation

Wichita State University · 2022

Led the two-year self-study and documentation submission that earned Wichita State University the Association of Public & Land-grant Universities' Innovation & Economic Prosperity (IEP) University Designation — placing WSU among approximately 90 IEP-designated institutions nationally. The designation recognizes universities with substantive, sustainable, institution-wide commitments to regional economic engagement across Talent, Innovation, and Place.

In 2023, WSU was named a finalist for the IEP University Awards in all three categories — Talent, Innovation, Place — and the top Connections award.
