How to engage MindScapes.

Seven ways to work with us — from a single keynote to a year-long build-and-launch engagement. Every engagement starts with a conversation about what you're trying to do and where MindScapes can add the most leverage.

Who MindScapes Works With

Organizations building across silos.

MindScapes works with leaders who recognize that single-discipline consulting isn't enough — and that the real work happens at the seams between infrastructure, workforce, and AI.

Enterprise

Mid-market and Fortune 500 organizations launching AI products, modernizing software platforms, or restructuring how their teams adopt new technology. Particularly: technology firms, manufacturers, and service companies undergoing AI-driven transformation.

Small & Growing Businesses

Founders and operators building AI into the operational core of their business — looking for strategy, tech-stack guidance, and a partner who understands both the technology and the team it takes to run it.

Higher Education & Workforce

Universities, community colleges, apprenticeship intermediaries, and workforce development organizations building AI-resilient credentials and partnerships with employers across multiple verticals.

Government & Public-Private Partnerships

State agencies, economic development organizations, and infrastructure consortiums standing up programs that require cross-sector convening — interconnection facilities, broadband expansion, regional workforce systems.

Funders & Conveners

Foundations, federal program officers, and grant-makers exploring complex multi-layer initiatives where the strategy depends on connecting actors across infrastructure, workforce, and AI.

Boards & Leadership Teams

Boards and C-suites navigating digital transformation decisions where a single advisor or vendor isn't enough — and where the right framing of the question matters more than the answer to it.

Engagement Types

Seven ways to work together.

Choose the engagement that fits where you are right now. Most clients start with one and evolve into another as the work develops.

01

Strategic Briefings

One-time deep dives for boards, leadership teams, or grant-makers exploring a complex integration question. A focused session — typically 90 minutes to a half day — that frames a decision, surfaces unseen layers, and leaves the team with a clear map of what to do next.

Best forDecision-stage clarity
Duration90 minutes to half day
OutcomeA clear next-step map
02

AI Strategy & Operations

For organizations integrating AI into how they actually run — strategy, solutions architecture, tech-stack selection, operational design, and product-market fit. We work with everyone from small businesses developing their first AI operational strategy to enterprises rethinking their entire stack. The deliverable is a working plan your team can execute, not a slide deck.

Best forBuilding AI into operations, not bolting it on
Duration30 days to multi-month
OutcomeExecutable AI strategy + operational design
03

Build & Launch

When the deliverable is a working product, not a recommendation. MindScapes assembles and runs the team that builds and launches AI-enabled software, platforms, or operational systems on aggressive timelines. We've delivered builds that internal teams projected would take a year in three months — and then trained the client's developers to maintain what was built using AI tools going forward.

Best forSoftware platforms, AI products, urgent timelines
Duration3 to 12 months
OutcomeA shipped product + a team trained to maintain it
04

Architecture Engagements

Multi-month projects to design and stand up integrated systems where infrastructure, workforce, and AI must work together. From interconnection facilities (IXPs) to apprenticeship programs to digital transformation initiatives — we architect what didn't exist before, recruit the partners required to operate it, and stay through launch.

Best forStanding up new programs or facilities
Duration6 to 18 months
OutcomeA running system with operational partners in place
05

Advisory Retainers

Ongoing relationships where MindScapes acts as connective tissue across a client's portfolio. Best for organizations expanding into new verticals, new geographies, or new markets where cross-sector relationships and AI-era strategy need to come from outside the building. Includes performance incentives where appropriate.

Best forSustained strategy + relationship leverage
Duration6 months to multi-year
OutcomeExpanded market reach + integrated portfolio strategy
06

Speaking & Workshops

Keynotes, panels, and intensive sessions for leadership teams, conferences, funders, and university audiences. Topics include data centers and digital infrastructure, AI-era workforce development, registered apprenticeships, change management, and the integrated transformation thesis. Half-day workshops are also available for executive teams that need more than a keynote.

Best forConferences, board offsites, executive sessions
FormatKeynote, panel, or half-day workshop
OutcomeAudience leaves seeing the layers they didn't see before
07

Coalition Building & Channel Strategy

When a single organization can't carry the work alone. MindScapes convenes the partnerships, channel networks, and public-private coalitions required to move large initiatives forward. Also includes channel strategy, customer development, and revenue-stream architecture for organizations that need a connector — not another salesperson — to open new markets.

Best forCross-sector initiatives + channel expansion
DurationProject-based or ongoing
OutcomeA coalition or channel that didn't exist before
