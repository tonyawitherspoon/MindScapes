Three Ways to Engage

Advisory. Build & Launch. Speaking & Writing.

Three ways to work with MindScapes. Each one covers a range of engagement depth and duration — the right fit gets worked out in the conversation.

01

Advisory

Strategic counsel — from a single board briefing to a multi-year retainer. MindScapes advises; your team executes. We work with leaders making decisions where a single-discipline consultant isn't enough, and where the right framing of the question often matters more than the answer.

Includes
  • Strategic briefings for boards, leadership teams, and grant-makers
  • AI strategy & operational design — for enterprises and small businesses alike
  • Solutions architecture, tech-stack guidance, and operations strategy
  • Product-market fit work for startups and entrepreneurs
  • Channel strategy, revenue-stream design, and customer development
  • Marketing & communications strategy
  • Event strategy, workforce training program design
  • Legislative briefings and expert testimony
  • Advisory retainers — ongoing portfolio counsel
Duration90 minutes to multi-year
Best forDecision-stage clarity & ongoing strategy
OutcomeA clear path forward your team can execute
02

Build & Launch

When the deliverable is a working thing, not a recommendation. MindScapes assembles and runs the team that builds and launches — software, programs, facilities, coalitions. We've stood up clinical-grade labs in 90 days, delivered AI software builds in three months that internal teams projected would take a year, and architected the first carrier-neutral IXP in Kansas.

Includes
  • AI-enabled software products and platforms
  • Operational systems and laboratories
  • Infrastructure facilities (interconnection points, broadband, data centers)
  • Registered apprenticeship programs and curriculum
  • Workforce pipelines and credential frameworks
  • Coalitions and channel networks where delivery requires convening
  • Developer training so internal teams can maintain the build
Duration3 to 18 months
Best forStanding up new products, programs, or facilities
OutcomeA shipped, running system with the team to sustain it

For Founders & Small Businesses

A doorway for early-stage and growing operators.

MindScapes works with small businesses through the same three engagement types — Advisory, Build & Launch, and Speaking & Writing — but the work itself often looks different. AI is creating capacity small teams could never afford before. Tonya helps founders and small business owners decide what to invest in, what to build, what to wait on, and how to design operations that scale without breaking.

Common Work
  • AI operational strategy — what to build, what to buy, what to skip
  • Tech stack design for businesses moving from solo to scaled
  • Product-market fit for early-stage founders
  • Channel strategy and revenue stream design
  • Marketing & communications guidance
  • CMMC certification support for SMBs in the DoD supply chain
Ecosystem Roles

Tonya serves on the Groover Labs Accel-KS Grant Evaluation Committee — interviewing 60+ proof-of-concept applicants and providing product-market fit and technology support to every grant winner. She's a founding leader of 1 Million Cups Wichita, a board member of NXTUS, a mentor in the Campfire series, and a regular speaker at WIBA. A meaningful portion of MindScapes' small business work is delivered pro bono through these channels.

Why this matters to MindScapes' bigger work: The small business practice isn't a side project — it's part of how MindScapes builds. Many of Tonya's team partners started as founders she advised, students she taught, or grant applicants she championed. The ecosystem is also the bench.
