Meet the connector.

For 30 years, Tonya Witherspoon has been building at the intersection of technology, workforce, and infrastructure — often years before the rest of the market caught up.

Tonya Witherspoon

FOUNDER & CEO · INNOVATOR · STRATEGIST · CONNECTOR

Tonya has spent 30 years doing this work from inside institutions — Wichita State University, NetApp, LSI Corporation, ESSDACK, and multiple companies she founded. She helped build Kansas's first carrier-neutral IXP. She co-founded the WSU Molecular Diagnostics Lab. She scaled university-industry partnerships to 700+ employers, contributing to WSU's #1 U.S. ranking in aerospace engineering R&D. Earlier, she led the digital transformation of WSU's Media Resources Center — moving television and radio to streaming years before it became standard.

MindScapes is the vehicle Tonya built so this work doesn't have to live inside one institution anymore. The current firm continues a thread first started in 2007, when Tonya founded the original MindScapes as a digital media, esports, and technology training center. Same mission. Larger scope. Three decades of relationships compounding into operational credibility.

"I'm a connector. I see the layers others can't, and I bring the right people to each one. That's been the through-line of my entire career."

Tonya is a featured speaker at the Rockefeller Foundation's Big Bets for America alongside Rajiv Shah, Heartland Forward President Angie Cooper, governors, and senior leaders from Google.org and Boeing. She has provided persuasive testimony before the White House Domestic Policy Council and the Kansas State Legislature. She's a published author of more than a dozen books on technology and education, a recurring contributor to Broadband Breakfast on national infrastructure policy, and a long-tenured mentor with Women Who Code.

Based in Wichita. Works nationally.
